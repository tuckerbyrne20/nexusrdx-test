Frequently asked questions

What data does Nexus RDX access?

The skills read the Gmail and documents you point them at to find and evaluate deals. They run inside your own Claude environment — your data isn't sent to a Nexus RDX server, and nothing is shared without your action.

How does pricing / access work?

Nexus RDX is currently in early access. The Claude skills on the Downloads page are free to add to your own Claude; for a guided rollout or custom underwriting criteria, get in touch.

What asset types are supported?

The underwriting and risk models cover the major commercial real estate types — multifamily, industrial, office, retail, and self-storage — across core, core-plus, value-add, and opportunistic strategies.

Do I need to be technical to use it?

No. Download a skill, add it to Claude, and ask in plain language — e.g. "run the broker pipeline" or "underwrite this OM." Claude handles the rest.

What do I get as output?

An Excel workbook with the full underwriting, plus an interactive HTML dashboard/map you can open in any browser — see the Product tab for a live example.
