Claude Skill

Broker Email Pipeline

Searches your Gmail for real estate broker emails, underwrites each new deal with institutional acquisition criteria, and produces an Excel workbook plus an interactive map. For unpriced deals, it recommends the offer price at which the asset becomes a good investment.

Download skill
Claude Skill

OM Underwriter

Reads uploaded PDF offering memoranda, extracts all available deal data, applies full institutional underwriting, and updates the shared Excel workbook and interactive map — recommending an offer price for unpriced deals.

Download skill
Claude Skill

Risk Scoring Framework

Scores CRE acquisitions across market, credit, physical, environmental, and execution risk — producing a composite 0–100 risk score with category breakdowns, dealbreaker escalations, and risk-adjusted return metrics. Can score a deal directly or scan your inbox and risk-score each new offering.
